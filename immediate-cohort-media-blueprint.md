# fleshsesh | academy — Immediate Cohort Media Blueprint

## Global treatment

The immediate cohort includes **FSH 105 and FSH 201–207**. Each course receives one quiet **eight-second, 16:9, silent visual transition** and one learner-triggered Australian-English narration track. The transition is a non-explicit editorial pause between reading moments, never a tutorial, a depiction of learners, or a required activity. There is no background music, no dialogue, no on-screen text, no autoplay, and no data collection.

The visual language is dark editorial with obsidian and deep-teal surfaces, blush-coral and soft-gold accents, soft directional lamplight, tactile paper and neutral study objects, and no visible people. Every visual keeps a still fallback and a textual transition description. Narration uses the existing warm, clear adult-education voice and appears with a readable transcript.
