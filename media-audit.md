## Replacement Design Baseline

The sources are present, but the rendered composition does not make them the experience. The welcome film is obscured by layered midnight overlays, the main library image is reduced to a narrow side panel, and the facility scenes read as thumbnails rather than destinations. The current large pastel faculty block also fractures the intended visual world. The replacement must foreground a full-bleed image-backed hero with an unmistakable video layer, elevate the campus imagery to navigable destinations, and sustain a single dark editorial visual language through the rest of the experience.
