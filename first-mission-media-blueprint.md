# fleshsesh | academy — First Mission Media Blueprint

## Purpose and learner controls

This initial media set supports the public Body Literacy first mission. It is an **optional sensory layer**, not an additional task: imagery introduces the topic, a short silent clip sets the learning atmosphere, and ambient audio is available only after an explicit learner action. The media must not auto-play, collect a viewing or listening history, require headphones, delay the mission, or change access, payment, enrolment, progress, reward, or disclosure data.

| Medium | Learner purpose | Control and fallback | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Editorial still image | Give the first mission a calm, adult-learning visual anchor. | Descriptive alternative text. | Non-explicit classroom and reference-material imagery only. |
| 8-second video | Offer a quiet establishing moment before the mission. | Explicit play/pause, muted by default, poster still, transcript/scene description, reduced-motion still-only fallback. | No dialogue, sexual activity, nudity, or identifiable learner data. |
| 60-second ambient audio | Offer an optional focus soundscape during self-paced learning. | Explicit play/pause and mute controls; never auto-plays. | Instrumental only; no spoken guidance, tracking, or listening record. |

## Visual definition

The visual sub-genre is a **cinematic editorial adult-learning environment**. It uses obsidian and deep-teal architecture, rose-lacquer accents, champagne-gold reference details, warm practical light, tactile paper, anonymous library and classroom objects, and diverse adult learners shown only in ordinary study contexts. No text, logos, watermarks, labels, clinical imagery, or explicit bodies may appear in generated assets.

## Audio blueprint

> Instrumental only, no vocals. Create a 60-second track at 72 BPM. Warm, unobtrusive ambient editorial music in D minor with soft felt piano, airy analog pads, restrained sub-bass, and no drums or percussion. The mood is calm, thoughtful, and privacy-first, with dark teal and champagne-gold tonal character, wide but gentle stereo space, and a clean low-volume studio mix. [0:00 - 0:20] sparse felt-piano chords and soft room tone, intensity 1/10. [0:20 - 0:40] add a quiet sustained analog pad and subtle low pulse, intensity 2/10. [0:40 - 1:00] remove the low pulse, return to spacious piano and pad, intensity 1/10. Avoid sudden changes, tension, and dramatic swells.
