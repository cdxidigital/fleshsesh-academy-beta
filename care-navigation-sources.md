# Care Navigation Source Record

This source record supports a non-clinical Australian care-navigation directory for **fleshsesh | academy**. It is not a diagnostic or crisis-service replacement. The learner-facing directory must use fixed external links, avoid forms and personal-data collection, and clearly state that emergencies require emergency services.

| Resource | Verified purpose | Learner-facing use | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Healthdirect mental-health helplines | Lists crisis, mental-health, violence, and other Australian support pathways; states that urgent medical help requires triple zero and provides Healthdirect’s 24/7 helpline | General urgent-support and mental-health navigation | https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/mental-health-helplines |
| Healthdirect Service Finder | Government-funded directory for healthcare professionals and services | Find nearby GP, urgent-care, hospital, and other public health services | https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/australian-health-services |
| Healthdirect sexual-health clinic finder | Location-based search for sexual-health clinics | Find a clinic without the academy requesting personal details | https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/australian-health-services/all-services/sexual-health-clinic/788122004 |
| 1800RESPECT | National 24/7 domestic, family, and sexual violence counselling, information, and support service; includes a quick-exit resource | Violence/abuse support and online-safety handoff | https://1800respect.org.au/ |

## Copy boundaries

- The directory may link to **000**, Healthdirect, Lifeline, 1800RESPECT, and Healthdirect service finders.
- The directory must not diagnose, assess symptoms, collect a location, request a disclosure, or replace emergency services.
- A quick-exit link may open 1800RESPECT’s established quick-exit resource; it must never claim to erase browser history.
- The directory must remain publicly available after the adult gateway only within the existing adult learning experience and may not send any care-choice data to the academy.
